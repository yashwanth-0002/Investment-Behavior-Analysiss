# Investment Behaviour Analysis

### Python | Data Analytics | Exploratory Data Analysis | Data Visualization

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on understanding investment behaviour, preferences, financial objectives and decision-making patterns using survey data collected from 40 respondents.

The analysis uses Python to explore survey responses, identify investment preferences, examine financial expectations and generate meaningful visualizations.

The objective is to transform raw survey data into understandable insights that can support better financial awareness and investment-related decision-making.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand respondents' investment preferences.
- Analyze the factors influencing investment decisions.
- Identify common financial and savings objectives.
- Examine expected investment returns.
- Understand investment monitoring habits and time horizons.
- Explore relationships between age and investment-related variables.

## 5. Project Workflow

The project follows these analytical steps:

1. Data Collection – Obtained survey responses.
2. Data Loading – Imported the Excel dataset using Pandas.
3. Data Inspection – Examined records, columns and data types.
4. Data Validation – Checked missing values and duplicate records.
5. Exploratory Data Analysis – Analyzed investment preferences and survey response distributions.
6. Statistical Exploration – Examined investment rankings, duration and numerical relationships.
7. Data Visualization – Created charts to communicate analytical findings.
8. Business Interpretation – Identified patterns and potential recommendations.

## 6. Key Analytical Findings

### A. Respondent Demographics

- Male respondents: 25 (62.5%)
- Female respondents: 15 (37.5%)
- Average respondent age: 27.8 years.

### B. Investment Avenue Selection

| Investment Avenue | Respondents | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mutual Fund | 18 | 45% |
| Equity | 10 | 25% |
| Fixed Deposits | 9 | 22.5% |
| Public Provident Fund | 3 | 7.5% |

**Key Finding:** Mutual Funds were the most frequently reported investment avenue, selected by 45% of respondents.

### C. Investment Decision Factors

| Factor | Respondents | Percentage |
|---|---:|---:|
| Returns | 25 | 62.5% |
| Risk | 14 | 35% |
| Locking Period | 1 | 2.5% |

**Key Finding:** Returns were the most frequently selected factor influencing investment decisions.

### D. Financial Objectives

- Capital Appreciation: 65%
- Growth: 27.5%
- Income: 7.5%

### E. Savings Objectives

- Retirement Planning: 60%
- Healthcare: 32.5%
- Education: 7.5%

### F. Expected Investment Returns

- 20–30%: 80%
- 30–40%: 12.5%
- 10–20%: 7.5%

**Key Finding:** Most respondents reported an expectation of returns in the 20–30% range.

### G. Investment Monitoring Habits

- Monthly: 72.5%
- Weekly: 17.5%
- Daily: 10%

### H. Investment Information Sources

- Financial Consultants: 40%
- Newspapers and Magazines: 35%
- Television: 15%
- Internet: 10%

Financial consultants were the most frequently reported information source.

## 7. Investment Ranking Analysis

The dataset contains ranking values for seven investment avenues.

The calculated average rankings are:

| Investment Avenue | Average Rank |
|---|---:|
| PPF | 2.025 |
| Mutual Funds | 2.550 |
| Equity Market | 3.475 |
| Fixed Deposits | 3.575 |
| Government Bonds | 4.650 |
| Debentures | 5.750 |
| Gold | 5.975 |

**Interpretation:** PPF has the lowest average ranking score. This indicates the strongest average preference only if the original questionnaire defined rank 1 as the highest preference and rank 7 as the lowest.

This ranking analysis is separate from the investment avenue selection question, where Mutual Funds received the highest number of responses.

## 9. Business Insights & Recommendations

Based on the descriptive findings:

1. Financial Awareness: Improve awareness of different investment avenues and their associated risks.
2. Investor Education: Provide educational resources explaining investment returns and risk considerations.
3. Retirement Planning: Develop financial awareness initiatives focused on long-term retirement planning.
4. Investment Guidance: Make reliable financial information accessible through different communication channels.
5. Investment Monitoring: Explore tools that help investors track their investments and financial goals.

These are potential recommendations derived from the survey patterns, not proven effects or predictions.

## 10. Limitations

- The analysis is based on only 40 survey respondents.
- Findings represent this sample and should not be generalized to the entire investor population.
- Expected returns represent respondents' expectations, not guaranteed investment performance.
- Survey-based responses may be influenced by individual perceptions.
- Correlation findings do not establish cause-and-effect relationships.
